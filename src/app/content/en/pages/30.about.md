I'm an Engineering Manager with over 15 years of experience combining hands-on engineering with people leadership: managing teams of up to 12, hiring and developing engineers, and aligning individual ambitions with team and organisational goals.
I find the greatest satisfaction not in my own output, but in creating the conditions for a team to do their best work: developing people, removing obstacles, and building a culture of trust and accountability.
This blog is where I share techniques and insights for building great software and great teams, inspired by real-world projects.
